# Work mode sound effects

Freesound previews (hq mp3), trimmed and peak-normalised to -1 dBFS. None are recordings of real apps; they are community sounds in the same style.

| file | used for | sound | author | licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| chat.mp3 | chat (Slack-style) card | [fast_knock.mp3](https://freesound.org/people/stwime/sounds/555530/) | stwime | CC0 1.0 |
| sync.mp3 | Teams-style chat card | [Message Receive](https://freesound.org/people/Froey_/sounds/760369/) | Froey_ | CC0 1.0 |
| mail.mp3 | mail card | [Message Sent](https://freesound.org/people/Froey_/sounds/760370/) | Froey_ | CC0 1.0 |
| ring.mp3 | incoming call loop (first 4 bars) | [Frutiger Aero Incoming Call Ringtone 04 "Southwinds"](https://freesound.org/people/Johnmode/sounds/818842/) | Johnmode | CC0 1.0 |
| decline.mp3 | declining a call | [VideoCallEnd.wav](https://freesound.org/people/aj_heels/sounds/634082/) | aj_heels | CC BY 4.0 (attribution required) |

Calendar, ticket and Humbl cards keep the synthesized sounds in `src/audio/audio.ts`.
